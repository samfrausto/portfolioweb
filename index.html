Welcome to the workshop

A room full of work.

The TV opens with Alma, an interactive learning experience. Hover over Cyberpunk or VR Baseball to watch its demo on the TV, or use the TV arrows to switch projects. Click the TV, a project model, or its label to open the same project viewer.

Browse the project list inside the viewer to move between work. The logo sculptures open my experience and the projects connected to it.

The three hardcover books on the worktable open Projects, Experience, and Skills. Hover to lift a cover. The “About & favorites” arrow takes you to a separate library. Its cabinet holds my favorite movies, songs, activities, and games. Select a shelf to explore it, or try Frosty’s hat on top.

Drag the wood from the rack into the fire for a little extra warmth. You can also tap “Feed the fire.” Drag elsewhere to look around. Escape closes a story.
