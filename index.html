About me

"I build things that live at the edge of what's technically possible and deeply human."

I'm a Biophysics major at USC, in the process of transferring into the new Human Technology Interaction program at the Iovine and Young Academy — a degree built for exactly the kind of work I do.

This summer I'm joining Edwards Lifesciences as an Extended Reality Learning Design Intern, bringing spatial computing into medical education. It's the exact intersection I've been building toward: XR with real clinical stakes.

My background in cancer immunology research shapes how I approach design — every project starts with a real person and a real problem.
