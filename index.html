The interactive workshop

Choose. Watch. Explore.

  1. Choose a project using the Alma, VR Baseball, or Cyberpunk buttons below the TV.
  2. Watch the TV. Alma shows a live beating heart. Baseball and Cyberpunk play short demonstrations. Use the Motion control to pause or resume animations.
  3. Open the story to try the experience, see my contribution, and explore the process.
More to discover around the room

You can also hover over a project on the shelves to preview it, or select its label to open the story. The company logos open my experience. The books open Work, Experience, and Skills.

Visit “My favorites” for films, music, games, and activities. Try Frosty’s hat, or add a log to the fire. Drag the room or use the arrows to look around.

Keyboard: use Tab to reach a control and Enter to select it. Escape closes a story.
