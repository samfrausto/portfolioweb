    Know a good hand.

    Make your best five-card hand from your two private cards and the five community cards. You can use both, one, or neither of your private cards.

      Aces go high or low. A–2–3–4–5 is the lowest straight. 10–J–Q–K–A is the highest. A straight cannot wrap around from king to ace to two.

      Same kind of hand? Compare the ranks that make the hand, then the remaining cards (kickers). Suits have equal value. Identical best five-card hands split the pot.
