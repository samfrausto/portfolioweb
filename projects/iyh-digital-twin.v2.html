A digital twin of USC's Iovine and Young Hall. My three-person sub-team built the delivery layer and waypoint navigation for Web, Meta Quest, and Apple Vision Pro.

The project

One building model, delivered on three platforms.

The class modeled the architecture and interiors of Iovine & Young Hall in NVIDIA Omniverse. My three-person sub-team owns the delivery layer.

We are making the shared twin walkable on the web, Meta Quest, and Apple Vision Pro from one source model.

The build

Navigation you already know how to use.

Rather than inventing a new way to move through a building, we borrowed one everybody has used: waypoint navigation, the way Google Maps walks you down a street.
