A score with a fixed meaning

x = (J₀ − Jₚ) / B
J is outstanding-trip time. B is the maximum clock exposure of the same requested cohort. x is an output in [−1,1].

Always show raw seconds per trip and guardrails. +1 and −1 are mathematical extremes, not promised reachable road states.

Two different kinds of “how many cars”

Initial packing y is a stock of cars on a defined road. Requested inflow q is cars per hour arriving at its boundary. A road that starts empty can still receive heavy demand.

z changes the ramp/mainline mixture at fixed total requests. Destination concentration is a separate later experiment.

4 November 2026 · Edition 1 · Codex for Sam · Design package, no penetration results

The question is how replacing more cars' controllers with one frozen ML policy changes everyone's journey burden at the I-110 merge. We repeat matched comparisons, include people waiting outside the road, and retain harms as well as benefits.

Proposed experiment protocol

Edition RM-1, 4 November 2026. Primary direction: Sam's A04 (source not included). Shared merge pressure first is user-confirmed; definitions below are proposed for protocol freeze. There are no measured penetration effects.

Question and estimands

How does whole-cohort burden change with policy share p under fixed exogenous conditions? Where does a mean gain hide ramp, human, tail or terminal harm? Do those conclusions change across factual-admissible human models and tested execution response?

For condition c and independent replication b, compare the same requested cohort under p=0 and p>0. Primary effect D(c,p,b) = [J(c,0,b) - J(c,p,b)] / N(c,b), in seconds saved per requested trip. Positive is favorable. Report the paired total vehicle-seconds as well. The primary conditional-checkpoint estimate averages independent block differences, with equal block weights. This is distinct from a request-weighted population estimate; report that separately if N varies.

The treatment includes the frozen actor, observation/message contract, normalization, action adapter and safety pipeline. Keep this package identical across p. Per-p retraining answers a different question. The no-ML arm must apply the declared untreated human/script model to every cohort car. The existing four-ACC fixture is a development reference, not automatically the human-only counterfactual.

Packing reference. Partition the declared road footprint into nonoverlapping usable lane segments. For homogeneous cars use N_jam_ref = sum floor(length_j / (vehicle_length_ref + standstill_gap_ref)), after removing unusable junction/taper storage and boundary clearance. This is a geometric standstill reference, not measured maximum flow or a promise every cell can initialize. Freeze the table of lengths/exclusions and reference car before p or closure comparisons. Mixed lengths require a declared equivalent-vehicle or explicit placement rule, not a policy-dependent denominator. At y=1 stopped gridlock is plausible; treat it as a stress test. If placement is invalid, status is structurally infeasible. Record actual y after integer rounding. Do not shrink the denominator when lanes close.

Separate load and merge mixture. Fix total new requests and their time profile while varying z; redistribute those requests between mainline and ramp with a saved keyed construction. This isolates origin mixture, not a fixed-mainline-plus-extra-ramp intervention. Save actual integer counts. z is undefined when no new requests exist. y=0 with q>0 starts empty but is not an empty-traffic test; y=q=0 is the genuinely empty sanity case. A single y cannot represent an open road's entire traffic state.

Destination extension. The local fixture has through, entry and Vernon-exit routes. For a named feasible exit, define target share d=N_target/N_eligible, with actual route counts and origin eligibility. d=0 means nobody eligible targets that exit; d=1 means everyone eligible does. It does not mean every trip has a unique destination. A concentration index over D exits could instead be (D sum s_j²-1)/(D-1); zero means equal exit shares, not absence of common destinations. Do not mix these definitions or call z a destination variable. Route reachability and path-length differences need a separate matched experiment before this extension.

Closure. Declare lane IDs in a stable physical-lane convention, closed segment and taper, time of closure/reopening, permission rules, speed-limit changes, obstruction geometry and route reachability. L refers to the selected cut, not the maximum lane count elsewhere; the I-110 auxiliary lane makes that distinction essential. For a=L, report no-throughput stress or reachable alternative exit behavior. Never delete requests or silently reroute them. Do not initialize cars inside an already-closed footprint: use a common valid pre-event state or declare that cell infeasible. A work-zone package with both closure and speed restriction is a package effect until separately ablated.

Complete burden and signed score

Let r_i be requested departure, clipped to zero for cars initially present. Use requests with r_i < H. Let c_i be completion at the declared destination; c_i=infinity when unfinished, removed, teleported without validated completion, or terminated. Then:

b_i(H) = min(c_i,H) - max(r_i,0)
J_H = sum_i b_i(H) = integral_0^H (N_requested - N_completed) dt
B_H = sum_i (H - max(r_i,0))
D_seconds = (J_H(0) - J_H(p)) / N
x_b = (J_H(0) - J_H(p)) / B_H

B_H is the maximum clock exposure of that exact manifest. It is fixed across the paired sweep. Thus 0 <= J <= B and -1 <= x <= 1. x=0 is no net burden change; positive is less outstanding time. +/-1 are mathematical extremes of the entire observation budget, not calibrated road-quality thresholds or generally attainable driving outcomes. Empty B yields null with reason empty_cohort. Even eliminating all baseline burden need not give +1 because J_0 may be much smaller than B.

This transparent scale avoids fitting extrema to whichever policy wins. Retain seconds per request, raw J and percentage 100(J_0-J_p)/J_0 (undefined at J_0=0). Example, arithmetic illustration only: B=10,000 vehicle-s, J_0=4,000, J_p=3,000 yields x=.10 and 25% less baseline burden. These are different denominators. Report mean paired x with its own interval; do not transform a mean D interval with a variable mean B.

Rejected alternatives: observed min/max normalization changes meaning when a new run appears; clipping percent improvements at -1 hides worsening beyond 100%; (J_0-J_p)/(J_0+J_p) is bounded but gives a different, compressed effect scale. No formula here is a validated societal utility score. The proposed B-normalized score is our derived visualization convention, not a published empirical finding.

Initial cars contribute residual time only. TripInfo arrival-only averages omit waiting/unfinished cases unless explicitly handled; use the request ledger as authority and SUMO unfinished/undeparted outputs as crosschecks. For exact integration use event times or an explicitly documented time-step convention; compare a hand-computable ledger. Split J into waiting, in-network and unresolved/removal residual burden. A removed car remains outstanding through H; never double-count its state.

Causal interpretation and comparisons

Random assignment changes the controller, which changes braking/gaps, insertion, queue formation and discharge, which change journey burdens. Geometry, traffic requests, traits, model and events influence these paths; pair them before outcomes. Do not adjust the primary effect for realized speed, insertion or coverage because those can be policy effects. Whole-traffic interference is intended: vehicles are not independent experimental units.

One-factor contrasts aid interpretation. A p-by-demand interaction is [D(p,high)-D(p,low)] on coupled exogenous draws, not two unrelated best-case curves. Cross p × q × z and closure in stages; one-factor-only sweeps miss interactions. Do not assume monotonic gains or interpolate a reliable threshold between six samples.

Keep same-p communicating versus separately trained local-information actors, equally tuned scripts, human-model uncertainty, untouched factual audit and bounded Isaac response checks when making the original stronger claims. Existing lane-action actors differ from the older longitudinal-only protocol; freeze an explicit action-scope amendment rather than silently treating them as equivalent. A p sweep alone establishes neither learning superiority nor communication value.

Rho: replication, precision and run budget

RM-1 proposal. No pilot variance or study runtime has been measured. Numerical examples below are planning illustrations, not simulation outcomes. Sam prefers to choose worthwhile-improvement thresholds after data are available; keep delta* open. A development-informed margin can be frozen for fresh confirmation. A final-data-informed margin is exploratory.

What rho counts

Use rho=n independent traffic-plus-assignment blocks per condition and checkpoint. Each block supplies one complete paired p sweep. A deterministic rerun is a reproducibility check, not another independent block. Different cars or timesteps within an episode are not independent traffic replications.

Keep R independent trainings separate. Use A independent assignment rankings per traffic manifest only when diagnosing placement variability; A rankings of one traffic trace share that trace and are not nA independent traffic blocks. If demand is built from observed days, cluster derived traces by the original day. More random seeds do not create more observed days.

A 20% assignment in one episode can be uniformly randomized and unbiased under its assignment design. It is not automatically statistically invalid; its uncertainty is simply not estimated by one episode. Exact counts induce dependence among car labels. They do not sample every possible assignment, and that is not required for valid Monte Carlo estimation.

Practical pilot recommendation

After interface and baseline gates, propose 20 independent development traffic blocks, A=2 rankings, at three untreated-selected conditions (light, congestion onset, congested), one fixed candidate checkpoint and six p levels. This costs 3 × 20 × [1 + (1 + 4×2)] = 600 unique executions, because p=0 and p=1 do not depend on assignment ranking. It yields 20 independent traffic clusters per condition, not 40. This is a practical starting pilot for variance/cost estimation, not a claim that 20 is sufficient. No new training is implicitly authorized.

First perform tiny no-ML, single-ML, all-ML and empty/lifecycle checks; those are separate engineering costs. Measure startup, logged episode plus drain time, failed-run recovery, bytes/run and memory under the intended workload. Report training variance as unknown until independent accepted training replicates exist.

For the first fixed-checkpoint confirmation use A=1 and fresh n blocks, averaging over the declared random assignment law. Use the pilot A=2 decomposition to decide whether more traffic or placements would help. Do not silently mix a balanced assignment pilot with an unrestricted-random final estimand.

Precision rule

For paired per-block D in seconds/request, estimate development SD s_D. Choose desired half-width h, confidence 1-alpha and family of m contrasts before final access. For one frozen checkpoint with independent blocks, choose the smallest n satisfying:

t_(1-alpha/(2m), n-1) * s_plan / sqrt(n) <= h

Iterate n because the Student-t quantile changes. Use a conservative planning SD, such as a variance-inflated pilot SD, and show s, 1.25s and 1.5s scenarios. The final interval uses the actual final SD. This is an approximate planning rule under independent blocks and suitable mean sampling behavior; skewed/gridlocked mixtures, rare events and small n require development resampling/design checks. It never guarantees final width or model validity.

FHWA's 2019 Alternatives Analysis recommends explicitly specifying precision and estimating replications, rather than adopting a fixed repeat count. Its older Appendix B lays out the t-interval iteration. Our adaptation uses paired differences, family control, separate pilot/final samples and no opportunistic final stopping.

Illustration: if s_plan=8 seconds and h=2 seconds, a single 95% interval needs about 64 blocks (calculator gives the exact integer). For the five p>0 comparisons within a condition, Bonferroni family m=5 raises the count to about 110. Halving h roughly quadruples cost. The saved planner table computes exact values for h=1,2,5 and s=4,8,12; no chosen h is attributed to Sam.

For a 54-cell core there are 9 conditions × 5 nonzero-p contrasts = 45 primary comparisons for one checkpoint. A separate claim about all 45 needs m=45, not five. Adding models, subgroups or interactions to the confirmatory family increases m; define family membership explicitly. Pointwise exploratory curves remain useful if labeled pointwise.

As a conservative sensitivity, since x is in [-1,1], Hoeffding plus a union bound yields n >= ceil[2 log(2m/alpha)/h_x²] for simultaneous error at most h_x under independent identically distributed blocks. This can be extremely costly and addresses Monte Carlo score precision only, not empirical credibility or independent training. It is a mathematical bound, not our default budget or an assurance that the chosen traffic model is correct.

For a two-sided test of a predeclared meaningful delta, a rough normal power screen is n ≈ [(z_(1-alpha/(2m)) + z_(1-beta)) s_D / delta]². Do not confuse power for rejecting zero with demonstrating equivalence. Do not use the observed final effect as delta to claim achieved power. Prefer effect intervals and practical margins.

Independent training uncertainty

For a crossed design, a useful planning approximation is:

Var(mean D) ≈ sigma_training²/R + sigma_traffic²/n + sigma_interaction²/(R*n)

Additional assignment variation nests within traffic; estimate it from A>1 and include it in the design model. Use equal training weights. For confirmation, independently resample training IDs and traffic/day IDs while keeping each full p/condition/arm vector paired; resample nested assignments within their parent if the final estimator uses them. Small R makes training-tail estimation unstable; compare per-training results and use a predeclared conservative interval method validated on pilot simulations. Never feed R*n rows into an ordinary independent t-test.

Candidates R={3,5,7}, n={20,40,80,160} are a budgeting grid, not evidence any count works. If one checkpoint is all that is available, label inference conditional on that checkpoint. No algorithm-level robustness claim follows.

Let C be distinct environment conditions, n traffic blocks, R trained checkpoints per learned arm, A assignment rankings, and K=6 distinct realized policy counts. One learned arm costs:

U = C*n * [1 + R*(1 + (K-2)*A)]

One p=0 is reused across trainings; p=1 is run once per checkpoint, because labels are identical across A. At C=9,n=40,R=5,A=1, U=9,360. At A=2, U=16,560, not twice 9,360. These count unique executions, not independent units or comparisons. Reuse requires identical complete input/config/runtime semantics, not a matching filename.

Two learned arms add R_L and R_C inside the bracket. Independently stochastic evaluation needs saved policy-noise streams; remove any reuse invalidated by stochastic semantics. Scripts at intermediate p add their own executions; no-ML remains reusable if identical. Per-car subgroup relabeling adds analysis records but no traffic runs. Horizon sensitivity reruns, Isaac cases, training, factual calibration and repairs each get explicit rows.

Illustrative compute only: 9,360 evaluations × 30 seconds = 78 serial service-hours; at 120 seconds each, 312 hours. At 5 MB/run, 46.8 GB decimal. These are assumed costs, not measurements. The calculator accepts actual measured seconds/bytes. Concurrency may shorten elapsed time only after a matching benchmark; do not divide already-aggregate throughput by workers again.

Freeze and stopping

Freeze n/R/A, family, h or clearly stated descriptive aim, maximum resource budget, failure rules, manifest IDs and analysis code before confirmation. Select n on pilot precision only; pilot cases remain development. No adding runs until significance. An exhausted budget yields an incomplete/low-precision result. A later continuation uses a logged amendment and fresh evidence or a valid predeclared sequential method.

No final numeric rho is scientifically determined yet. The immediate proposal is 20 pilot blocks per selected condition, with final rho computed from measured variance and an explicitly chosen precision objective.

Status vocabulary

Use separate readiness, execution and value status fields:
- readiness: proposed, unsupported, qualified, structurally_infeasible;
- run_status: not_run, running, completed, acceptance_failed, engineering_failed, cancelled;
- value_status: observed, not_run, not_applicable, missing, invalid;
- partition: development, confirmation, factual_audit, illustration.

A completed run can fail a guardrail. A failed run must still be visible. No-observation, empty subgroup, missing sensor and numerical zero are different. A blocked closure is not a missing value if its burden was measured. No synthetic demonstration enters the empirical outcomes table.

Paired manifests

Before any p evaluation, save canonical sorted UTF-8 JSON (finite values, stable key order) for:
1. network/topology/source hashes, physical lane mapping and initial state;
2. the complete requested departure/route/origin/trait table, even for cars that never enter;
3. event and downstream-supply schedules;
4. human model, safety pipeline, rates, H, drain and warm-up;
5. independent traffic, assignment, simulator and evaluation-policy RNG keys.

All six p runs reference the same exogenous hash. Checkpoint, normalization and safety hashes also match within a checkpoint sweep. Controller masks and label-derived metadata are stored separately. The pairing validator rejects mismatched condition, block, traffic hash, cohort hash, H or safety contract.

Use immutable output folders: outputs/penetration-study/<protocol>/<condition>/<traffic>/<checkpoint>/<assignment>/<p>/<attempt>. Preserve every failed attempt and a supersedes link for repaired engineering runs. Never overwrite the original.

Assignment: sort IDs canonically, then permute with a recorded seed and RNG implementation. For each p, select the first floor(pN+.5) IDs. This is uniform sampling without replacement at each k and nested across p. Save the realized ranking, not only its seed. Different p values that round to the same k are aliases, not independent conditions. At N=0, k=0 and actual share is null.

The executable utility supplies this uniform-random law. Route/time balance is optional: predefine strata independent of outcomes, randomize within each, then choose the stratum with greatest proportional allocation deficit at each rank (seeded tie-break). Record actual deviations; such a constrained law is not unrestricted uniform assignment. A clustered placement is another declared law. Never handpick a good ranking.

For A>1, save new independent rankings under the same traffic_id. Baseline and all-ML runs reuse only when every effective setting is identical. Preserve subgroup memberships as analysis links rather than rerunning unchanged traffic.

Hand-checkable example, not a simulation result

At H=10, two requests start at t=0 and t=4. The first completes at t=6; the second remains outside. J=6+6=12 vehicle-seconds; B=10+6=16; N=2. If treatment completes the first at 5 but still leaves the second outside, J=11, D=.5 s/request and x=1/16. Dropping the outside request would falsely change the population. The unit checks use this exact construction.

Required validations before accepting a run

Reconcile requested = completed + outstanding, with outstanding split into mutually exclusive external/active/unresolved states. Reconcile J to the per-trip ledger and its decomposition. Verify k/N, actual rank membership, origin counts and no disappeared requests. Check every timestamp and quantity is finite and within its declared domain; null carries a reason. Check 0<=J<=B, raw/hash identity and complete cohort equality across pairs.

Validate N=0; p=0; p=1; tiny N and duplicate k; no ramp; all ramp; no active ML; delayed admissions; departures/arrivals in one step; early termination; all lanes blocked; invalid route; failed drain; and a numeric zero result. Treat cell feasibility separately from outcome success.
