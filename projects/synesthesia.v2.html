Synesthesia

An ambient spatial app meant to run quietly in the background while you work — a living environment, not another window to manage.

The problem

Most spatial apps demand attention. This one should disappear.

Synesthesia was built as an experiential visionOS app that sits behind your work — changing color, music, and opacity without becoming a task. Teammates owned modeling and design; I owned the mechanics that made the ambient loop feel continuous.

The build

Controls that stay out of the way.

01

Scene switching

Mechanics to move between multiple ambient environments without breaking the sense that the world is still "on."

02

Color, music, opacity

Live controls for palette, audio, and AR opacity so the experience can sit at any intensity behind real work.

03

Unity → visionOS pipeline

Brought the Unity build onto visionOS with custom gradient shaders tuned for soft, ambient presence.

04

Beautiful at any opacity

Designed so the scene still reads when nearly transparent — ambient by default, not only when fullscreen.

What it proved

Spatial interfaces don't have to compete for focus — they can support the room you're already in.
