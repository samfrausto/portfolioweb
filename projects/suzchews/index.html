Two of the most important people in my life are cancer survivors.
They didn't lose their hair first. They lost their appetite.

Dysgeusia.

The taste disorder that turns scrambled eggs into metal - and a gum designed to give taste back.

“I knew I would feel horrible from the chemo, but I thought I’d at least be able to taste my scrambled eggs in the morning.”
