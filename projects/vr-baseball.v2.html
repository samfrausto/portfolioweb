The project

Repeatable catcher training without a live pitcher.

The prototype explores repeatable catching practice without a live pitcher. Headset performance and training benefit have not been verified.

I designed the controls and integrated pitch selection, trajectories and strike-zone detection in Unity. AI generated all code.

The build

Make a pitch repeatable.

Pitch profiles control speed and break so the same catching situation can be explored repeatedly. The prototype’s physics accuracy has not been verified.
